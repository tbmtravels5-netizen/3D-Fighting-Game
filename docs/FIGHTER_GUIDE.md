# Fighter Guide

## 6 Playable Fighters - Version 1.0

### ⚡ Raiden - Lightning Specialist
**Playstyle**: Fast, ranged, combo-heavy

- Health: 95
- Speed: 9/10
- Damage: 8/10
- Defense: 7/10

**Special Move**: Lightning Chain
- Multiple quick hits with electric effect
- Stuns opponent temporarily
- Cost: 30 stamina

**Ultimate**: Thunderbolt Finale
- Massive lightning attack
- Area damage around impact
- Cost: 100 stamina

---

### 🔥 Phoenix - Fire Specialist
**Playstyle**: Balanced, area damage, sustained pressure

- Health: 100
- Speed: 7/10
- Damage: 8/10
- Defense: 8/10

**Special Move**: Inferno Blast
- Fire wave that burns opponent
- Continuous damage over time
- Cost: 30 stamina

**Ultimate**: Phoenix Rising
- Rise in flames, crash down
- Massive area damage
- Cost: 100 stamina

---

### ❄️ Frost - Ice Specialist
**Playstyle**: Defensive, crowd control, slow-down effects

- Health: 98
- Speed: 6/10
- Damage: 7/10
- Defense: 9/10

**Special Move**: Frozen Shards
- Launches ice projectiles
- Slows opponent movement
- Cost: 30 stamina

**Ultimate**: Absolute Zero
- Freeze opponent in place
- Build up for powerful counter-attack
- Cost: 100 stamina

---

### 🌑 Shadow - Speed Specialist
**Playstyle**: Quick combos, evasive, high risk/high reward

- Health: 85
- Speed: 10/10
- Damage: 7/10
- Defense: 6/10

**Special Move**: Shadow Clone
- Briefly confuse opponent
- Land guaranteed hit
- Cost: 30 stamina

**Ultimate**: Shadow Assassin
- Multiple rapid strikes
- High damage on full combo
- Cost: 100 stamina

---

### 💪 Titan - Power Specialist
**Playstyle**: Heavy damage, slow, overwhelming force

- Health: 110
- Speed: 5/10
- Damage: 10/10
- Defense: 9/10

**Special Move**: Earthquake Stomp
- Ground slam that knocks down
- Area of effect damage
- Cost: 30 stamina

**Ultimate**: Titanic Crush
- Devastating single blow
- Highest damage in game
- Cost: 100 stamina

---

### 🌀 Echo - Balanced Fighter
**Playstyle**: Versatile, adaptable, learner-friendly

- Health: 100
- Speed: 7/10
- Damage: 8/10
- Defense: 8/10

**Special Move**: Echo Strike
- Attack that repeats
- Scales with combo count
- Cost: 30 stamina

**Ultimate**: Infinite Echo
- Chain of powerful strikes
- Damage increases per hit
- Cost: 100 stamina

---

## Fighter Selection Strategy

**For Beginners**: Choose **Echo** - balanced stats, easy to learn
**For Speed Players**: Choose **Shadow** or **Raiden** - fast combos
**For Tank Players**: Choose **Titan** or **Frost** - defense-focused
**For Balanced Play**: Choose **Phoenix** - jack-of-all-trades
